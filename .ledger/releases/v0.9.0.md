# Ledger v0.9.0

## Summary

The reader looks like Dossier. The static reader and the public changelog
take Dossier's plum-tinted neutrals, its teal, violet, and coral status
colors, its system font stacks, radii, and motion, and keep Ledger's emerald
accent. The masthead is compact, wide screens put the facet rail on the left,
and the theme toggle cycles Auto, Light, and Dark. Rendered readers are
smaller because their JSON sidecars are compact.

## Public Notes

- The static reader and the public changelog use Dossier's visual system in light and dark themes: plum-tinted neutrals, borders in place of shadows, system font stacks with no font requests, and one motion curve. Ledger's emerald accent and mark stay.
- Color marks status only. Landed, released, accepted, and shipped records are teal. Drafts, planned, proposed, and in-progress work, active sessions, and captured notes are violet. Blocked and rejected records are coral, and the rest are neutral. Kind badges are neutral outline chips, and superseded, deprecated, and expired records show a neutral dot instead of a red one.
- The masthead is shorter, so records start higher on the page. From 1100 pixels wide, the facet rail sits on the left with a label over each group.
- The theme toggle cycles Auto, Light, and Dark, shows the current choice, and can return to following the system setting.
- Muted text, the search placeholder, and warning text in the light theme now meet 4.5:1 contrast, and text inputs and selects have a 3:1 edge. The record list no longer scrolls sideways at tablet widths.
- The search index, its shards, `graph.json`, and `graph/contracts.json` are written as compact JSON, and search shards fill up to `render.budgets.maxSearchIndexBytes`, passing it only when a single record is larger. Before, a shard of long records could exceed the budget and fail `ledger doctor`.
